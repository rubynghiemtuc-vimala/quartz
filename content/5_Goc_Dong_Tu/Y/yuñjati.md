# yuñjati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
yuñjati = √yuj+(↑ṃ)a (P.57)+ti (jṃ>ṃj, ṃj>ñj-P.49)

Syn.: 1) anuyuñjati, āsevati, paṭisevati, samāpajjati; 2) parakkamati; 3)gāheti; 4) codayati, niyuñjati, niyojayati

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: yutta = √yuj+ta (1)jt>tt, 2) j,t>tt-P.560/P.614, 3) j-P.584&586, t>tt-P.40)

K.578, 560. Bhuj’ādīnam’anto no dvi ca. Bhujaiccevamādīnaṃ dhātūnaṃ anto no hoti, tapaccayassa ca dvitāvo hoti. Bhutto, bhuttāvī, catto, satto, ratto, yutto, vivitto.

P.614.Vassa vā v’u…Yuja yoge, ayuñjīti yutto, yuttā, yuttaṃ

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) joins (in); engages (in); participates (in); practices

2) applies oneself (to); endeavours (in); makes effort (with); tries (to)

3) gets back; regains

4) encourages; urges; impels (to do)
