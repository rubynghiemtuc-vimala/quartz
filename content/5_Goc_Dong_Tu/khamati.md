# khamati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
khamati = √kham+a+tiAnt.: nakkhamati; Syn.: 1) adhivāsayati, adhivāseti, abhisambhavati, titikkhati, sahati; 2) upapajjati; 3) khametiCaus.: khamāpeti = √kham+ṇāpe+ti

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: khanta = √kham+ta (ta>nta,m-P.618/K.584)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) endures; tolerates; bears; withstands2) seems good (to); is acceptable (to); is suitable (for); is pleasing (to)3) pardons; accepts the apology (of); forgives
