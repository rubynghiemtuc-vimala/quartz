# nassati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
nassati = √nas+ya+ti (sy>s>ss-P.511)

Syn.: 1) panassati; 2) riccati; 4) antaradhāyati, apeti, paṭiveti, vinassati, vibhoti, veti

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: naṭṭha =  √nas+ta (st>ṭṭh-P.626)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) perishes; ends; is destroyed

2) is lost (by); is forgotten (by)

3) is squandered; is wasted; comes to naught; 4) disappears; vanishes; goes away
