# pasīdati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
pasīdati = pa+√sad>√sīd (K.609)+a+ti

Syn.: 2) pakkhandati; 3) abhinandati, modati, vippasīdati; 4) sannisīdati

Caus.: pasādeti = pa+√sad+ṇe+ti (a>ā)

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: pasanna = 1) pa+√sad+na (dn>nn)

2) pa+√sad+ta>anna (P.631)

P.631. Bhid’ādito inna-anna-īṇā vā….Annādese chada apavāraṇe, acchādīyitthāti channo, paṭicchannaṃ gehaṃ, pasīdīti pasanno.

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) is bright; is calm; becomes clear

2) gains confidence; is inspired

3) is pleased; is happy; is appeased

4) settles down; subsides; calms down
