# virundhati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
virundhati = vi+√rudh+(↑ṃ)a (P.57)+ti (dhṃ>ṃdh,ṃdh>ndh-P.49)

Pass.: virujjhati = vi+√rudh+ya+ti (dhy>jjh-P.511)

Syn. of

Pass.: nivārayati, nivāreti, vāreti

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: viruddha = vi+√rudh+ta (t>dh-P.607/K.576,dh>d-P.608/K.611)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) obstructs; prevents; 2) prohibit and surround; 3) torture.; 4) cover or conceal

Pass.: is blocked (by); is obstructed (by); is opposed (by)
