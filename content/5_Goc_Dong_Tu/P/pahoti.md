# pahoti

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
pahoti = pa+√bhū>√hū+a+ti (ū>o-P.489)

Ant.: nappahoti;

Syn.: 1) pabhavati, sambhoti; 2) ussahati, sakkoti, samattha, sambhoti

Pa.(III,s): pabahosa = pa+ba√bhū>√hū+a+(s)a/ttha (ū>a-P.463, ū>o-P.489), pbabhava = pa+ba√bhū+a+ti (ū>o,o>av-K.513)

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: pahūta = pa+√bhū>√hū+ta; pabhūta = pa+√bhū+ta

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) comes into being (from); arises (from); proceeds (from); is a product

2)is able (to); is capable (of); is qualified (to)

3) is sufficient (for); is enough (for)
