# jāyati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
jāyati = √jan+ya+ti (an>ā-P.511/K.444)

Syn.: 1) jāyate; 2) jāyatī, pabhavati, sañjāyati, sambhoti

P.511. Tathā kattari ca… Janajanane, “janādīnamā timhi cā”ti ettha “janādīnamā”ti yogavibhāgena yamhi dhātvantassa ākāro. Jāyati, jāyanti. Jāyate, jāyante

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: jāta = √jan+ta (an>ā-P.511) (a>ā-P.619/K.585, n-P.600/K.586)???

KPK.585, 619. Jan’ādīnam’ā-timhi ca. Janaiccevamādīnaṃ dhātūnaṃ antassa byañjanassa āttaṃ hoti tapaccaye pare, timhi ca. Ajanīti jāto, jananaṃ jāti

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) is born (in); comes into being (from)

2) arises (for); is produced (for)
