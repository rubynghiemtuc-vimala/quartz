# maññati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
maññati = √man+ya+ti (ny>ñ>ññ-P.447/K.441 or P.511) (= ñāṇe)P.511. Tathā kattari ca… Mana ñāṇe, ñādesova viseso, maññati, maññanti iccādi.Syn.: 1) āsaṅkati, vicinteti, saṅkappeti; 3) adhipajjatidahati, dahāti, dhārayati, paccavekkhati, samanupassati Caus.: māneti =  √man+ṇe+ti (a>ā)

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: mata =  √man+ta (n-P.600)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) thinks; imagines; conceives; presumes; supposes; 2) knows; is convinced; is sure;3) considers (to be); takes as; regards as; views as; deems as
