# āharati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
āharati = ā+√har+a+ti

Syn.: 1) ānayati, āneti, āvahāti; 3) uppādayati, uppādeti, janeti, nipphādeti, pasavati, pātukaroti, savati; 4) bhuñjati

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: āhaṭa = ā+√har+ta (r, t>ṭ-P.632/K.587)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) brings (for); brings back (for); fetches (for); procures (for)

2) (of negligence) manifests; produces; displays

3) generates; produces

4)  (of food) eats; uses; enjoys

5) nourishes; feeds
