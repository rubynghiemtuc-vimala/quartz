# abhisajati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
abhisajati = abhi+√saj+a+ti

Ref. sajati => √sajj

Pass. of abhisajati: abhisajjati = 1) abhi+√saj (P.608)+ya+ti (jy>j>jj-P.511)

Ant.: nābhisajjati;

Syn.: laggati

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**

(1) jt>tt; 2) j,t>tt-P.560; 3) j-P.584&586, t>tt-P.40)

2) abhisaṭṭha =  abhi+√saj+ta (1) jt>ṭṭh; 2)t>ṭṭh-P.626)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
reviles; offends

Pass.: 1) is overly attached (to); is overly fond (of); 2) loses one’s temper; curses angrily; abuses; swears; (comm) sticks
