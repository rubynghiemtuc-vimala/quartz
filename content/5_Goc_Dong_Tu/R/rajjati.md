# rajjati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
rajjati = rañjati = rajati

rañjati = √raj+(↑ṃ)a (P.57)+ti (jṃ>ṃj, ṃj>ñj-P.49)

rajjati = √raj+ya (Sad-dhā,p.46)+ti (jy>j>jj-P.511)

Ant.: virajjati;

Syn.: sārajjati

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: ratta = √raj+ta (1)jt>tt; 2) j, t>tt-P.560, 3) j-P.584&586, t>tt-P.40)

K.578, 560. Bhuj’ādīnam’anto no dvi ca. Bhujaiccevamādīnaṃ dhātūnaṃ anto no hoti, tapaccayassa ca dvitāvo hoti. Bhutto, bhuttāvī, catto, satto, ratto, yutto, vivitto.

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) finds pleasure (in); is enamoured (with); is infatuated (with); is attached (to)

2) takes pleasure (in); finds pleasure
