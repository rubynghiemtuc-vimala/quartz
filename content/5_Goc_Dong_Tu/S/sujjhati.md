# sujjhati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
sujjhati = √sudh+ya+ti (dhy>jjh-P.511)

(passive form but there is no active form)

Syn.: parisujjhati, visujjhati

Caus.: sodheti = √sudh+ṇe+ti (u>o-P.489); sodhayati

Syn.: 1) punāti, visodhayati, visodheti

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: suddha = √sudh+ta (t>dh-P.607/K.576,dh>d-P.608/K.611)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
is purified; is cleansed; is washed away

Caus.: 1) cleans; clears; purifies; caused to be pure; 2) corrects, clears a debt; 3) clears up; clarifies; 4) cleans up; tidies up
