# upeti

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
upeti = upa+√i+a+ti (i>e)

Ant.: nopeti, apeti

Syn.: 1) abhikkamati; 2) voyuñjati; 3) abhijānāti, āpajjati, nigacchati, paṭilabhati, paṭisevati, pappoti, vindati; 5) yujjati

Pa.: không có

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: upeta = upa+√i+a+ta (i>e)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) comes; approaches; enters

2) enters; gets involved (with); engages (in)

3) obtains; gets; experiences; suffers

4)  is suitable (for); is ready (for); is fit (for)

5) applies; is relevant
