# paṭippassambhati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
paṭippassambhati = pati+pa+√sambh+a+ti (pati>paṭi-P.43,p>pp & s>ss-P.40)

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**


**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) softens; collapses down; sags; slumps

2) calms down; relaxes; becomes settled

3) (of an illness) recedes; subsides; disappears; 4)  is annulled; is lifted; is no longer in force
