# bandhati

**Động từ / Gốc động từ (Present Tense & Irreg. cases; Caus., Pass.):**
bandhati = √badh+(↑ṃ)a (P.57)+ti (dhṃ>ṃdh,ṃdh>ndh-P.49)

Syn.: 1) ussineti; 2) sajjeti

Pass.: bajjhati = √badh+ya+ti (dhy>jjh-P.511)

**Các trường hợp bất quy tắc (Irreg. cases of AK, PK, a-a, t-p):**
AK: baddha = √badh+ta (t>dh-P.607/K.576,dh>d-P.608/K.611)

**Ý nghĩa (Meaning):**
1) binds; ties up; imprisons; confines

2) prepares;readies;equips;sets up

3) combines;forms;unites;gets together

4) is fixated (on); is obsessed (with)

5) captures; captivates; inspires

6) ties on, composes; 7) halts a caravan
